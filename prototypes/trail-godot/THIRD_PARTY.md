## Windtrail Sans

`assets/fonts/TrailSans.ttf` is a renamed, static 500-weight subset of Noto Sans SC, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It contains the demo's Chinese interface text, Latin characters and punctuation.

- Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
- Original font: `NotoSansSC[wght].ttf`
- License and copyright: [assets/fonts/OFL.txt](assets/fonts/OFL.txt)
- Modifications: variable font instantiated at weight 500; glyphs subset to the UI; family renamed to Windtrail Sans.

When adding Chinese UI text, regenerate the subset or use the original font with its license. Godot's OS font fallback is enabled, but bundled coverage makes the existing interface portable.
